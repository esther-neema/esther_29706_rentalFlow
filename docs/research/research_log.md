# Research Log: Car Rental Business in Rwanda

**Project idea:** A car rental system for a business in Rwanda. Customers can rent cars for a few hours, days, or weeks. The system keeps records of cars, customers, bookings, and payments, so the owner can see which cars are rented most and how much money the business earns.

## Summary of the gaps

1. **No local data on car rental.** Rwanda has more and more vehicles and visitors (sources 3 and 4), but there are no public figures on car rental itself (sources 3, 4, 6). A business has to collect its own data.
2. **Small businesses still use paper.** Small rental companies often use paper notes and phone calls, which causes booking mistakes and lost records (source 1).
3. **Existing systems lack owner reports.** The systems studied focus on booking, but give weak or no reports on the most rented cars and total income (sources 1 and 2).
4. **Rental time is not flexible.** Existing systems use full days or half days, not hours, days, and weeks (sources 1 and 5).
5. **Payment methods do not fit Rwanda.** Systems use credit cards, cash, or QR codes, but not mobile money (sources 1, 2, 5).
6. **The database must enforce the rules.** Oracle constraints and analytic functions (sources 7 and 8) can keep the records correct and produce the reports the owner needs.

These gaps support our project: a simple system for a Rwandan car rental business that supports hourly, daily, and weekly rentals, records payments (including mobile money), and gives the owner reports on the most rented cars and income.
